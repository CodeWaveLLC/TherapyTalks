Our roots

Kristina is a parent of a child with special needs and personally knows the challenges families face across every setting. That experience drives everything at Therapy Talks: families are listened to and included in the entire process, from evaluation through ongoing treatment.

A respectful, nurturing environment where children and families grow together, with the highest quality pediatric intervention in your child’s natural environment.

Our belief

The Charleston Chew program

When your child needs more than one session a week

Is your child tube-fed, or has a doctor mentioned moving in that direction? Worried about growth from not eating enough by mouth? We’re here to help with all feeding and swallowing concerns, including infants and children with medically fragile conditions.

2 weeksper intensive block
10 daysof consecutive therapy
45–60′two sessions each day

What the intensive includes

  • Two 45–60 minute feeding and swallowing sessions, daily for 10 days over a two-week period
  • Vital Stimulation – NMES training and additional oral-motor techniques, led by Kristina Behrens, owner of both Therapy Talks and Charleston Chew
  • Treatment focused on optimizing independence, setting medically fragile infants and children up for long-term success
  • A caring partner at every step. Questions about tube-feeding transition or growth are welcome before you even book a consult

Meet the team

Clinicians who live this work

Every clinician is a licensed, certified speech-language pathologist with experience across schools, homes and clinics.

Kristina Behrens

MS, CCC-SLP · Founder & Owner

Kristina began her career as a school-based speech-language pathologist serving children from preschool through high school, then moved through rehabilitative clinics to home health, where she supported infants with mild to the most severe impairments. Her expertise in medically fragile populations and feeding and swallowing disorders led her to Therapy Talks.

Hannah Steward

MS, CCC-SLP · Speech-Language Pathologist

Alison Hanno
