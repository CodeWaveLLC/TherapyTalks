About Therapy Talks

Built by a parent, for families who need more than an office visit

Our Roots

Kristina is a parent of a child with special needs and personally experiences the challenges families face across every setting. At Therapy Talks, that experience drives everything: families are listened to and included in the entire process — from evaluation through ongoing treatment.

Kristina Behrens · MS, CCC-SLP

Our Mission

A respectful, nurturing environment where children and families grow together — the highest quality pediatric intervention services, delivered in your child's natural environment.

Our Belief

All children can grow, learn and develop with the right guidance and support. We take a personalized, child-directed approach — applying evidence-based methods while children are engaged in play and exploration, maximizing function and independence at school, home and in the community.

What we do

Specialized pediatric therapy, two ways in

Our licensed and certified speech-language pathologists provide comprehensive evaluation and treatment to a diverse population of children — with family involvement as an essential part of every plan.

Speech-Language Therapy

Diagnostic, therapeutic and academic speech-language services in your child's natural environment — home, daycare or preschool. Personalized treatment plans developed with family, caregivers and other therapists.

Feeding & Swallowing

We help with all feeding and swallowing concerns — dysphagia, feeding-tube dependency, oral-motor chewing difficulty, “picky” eating habits, and food or oral aversions. From first contact through discharge, your family gets exceptional personal care.

The Charleston Chew program

When your child needs more than one session a week

Is your child tube-fed — or has a doctor mentioned moving in that direction? Concerned about growth from lack of nutritional intake by mouth? We're here to help with ALL of your feeding and swallowing concerns, including infants and children diagnosed with medically fragile conditions.

2weeks per intensive block

What the intensive includes

  • Two 45–60 minute feeding & swallowing sessions, daily for 10 days over a two-week period
  • Vital Stimulation – NMES training and additional oral-motor techniques led by Kristina Behrens, owner of both Therapy Talks and Charleston Chew
  • Focused on optimizing independence — top-of-the-line treatment that sets medically fragile infants and children up for success
  • A caring partner at every step: questions about tube-feeding transition or growth are welcome before you even book a consult
Meet the team

Clinicians who live this work

Every clinician is a licensed, certified speech-language pathologist with experience across schools, homes and clinics.

Kristina Behrens MS · CCC-SLP

Kristina began her career as a school-based speech-language pathologist, providing speech, language and swallowing services to children from preschool through high school — ranging from severe medical and cognitive challenges to advanced students with mild impairments. Her passion for medically based speech pathology led her through rehabilitative clinics to home health, where she supported infants from mild to the rarest and most severe impairments.

Her expertise in medically fragile populations, feeding and swallowing disorders — plus her own experience as a parent of a child with special needs — led her to found Therapy Talks, alongside Charleston Chew Intensives. She is trained in Vital Stimulation – NMES.

Founder & Owner

Hannah Steward MS · CCC-SLP

Hannah earned her bachelor's degree in Speech and Hearing Science from Delta State University (2022) and her master's degree in Speech-Language Pathology from Southern Illinois University – Carbondale (2024). She has experience working with children across schools, homes and outpatient clinics.

Speech-Language Pathologist

Alison Hanno MS · CCC-SLP

Alison has over 15 years of experience in speech-language pathology. Originally from Massachusetts, she studied at Boston University and earned her master's at Mass General Hospital Institute of Health Professionals. After years in skilled nursing as both therapist and manager, she found a new passion for the 0–3 population.

Alison loves teaching families and other professionals how a speech therapist can help children with swallowing and feeding difficulties — and is happily settling into life in South Carolina away from her husband's home state, with travel, puzzles, biking and kayaking on the agenda.
